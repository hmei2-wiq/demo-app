# Wedge

Status: LOCKED (approved at checkpoint 1, 2026-11-04)

**Wedge sentence:** FreshCart lets a busy household rebuild its weekly grocery shop in under two minutes by reordering a past basket or a saved list, then booking a pickup or delivery slot.

```yaml
product: FreshCart            # original fictional brand; NOT a Woolworths-branded app
scale_tier: preview
product_type: saas
icp: Busy households in Australian metro areas who buy a similar basket every week
axis: Speed of repeat shopping (reorder and lists)
anti_axis: Product discovery and recipes
stack_flags:
  mobile_first: true
  realtime: false
  ai_heavy: false
  regulated: false
  multi_tenant_b2b: false
  eu_global_billing: false
  data_heavy: false
```

## Why this wedge
Grocery shops are mostly repeat purchases. The slow steps in existing apps are rebuilding the basket and finding a slot. Reorder, lists and a clear slot picker attack both.

## Out of wedge (v2, Priority C)
Recipes, personalised recommendations, real payments, loyalty points, substitutions chat, store locator.

## Research caveat
No web market research was run for this draft (no analyst phase with live sources); the wedge rests on general knowledge of grocery apps and should be treated as an assumption until checked.
